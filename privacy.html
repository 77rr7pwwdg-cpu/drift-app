Privacy Policy

Drift · Last updated November 15, 2026

Drift is a music player made by Serafim Kerentsev, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Drift.

The short version

Drift does not collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your songs, playlists and settings stay on your device, and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account.

What stays on your device

To work, Drift saves these things on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Songs you add, and their details: title, artist, cover, favorites and playlists.
  • Your settings: speed, effects, repeat, sort order, layout and the song you were on.
  • A small snapshot of the current song (title, artist, cover, and whether it’s playing) for the Drift widget on your Home Screen.

This information is never sent to the developer. Deleting Drift deletes it from your device.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Drift uses Apple's iCloud to keep your library, songs, playlists and settings the same on your devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer cannot see or access it. Synced songs count toward your iCloud storage. You can turn sync off at any time in Drift; the copy in your iCloud account stays there until you remove it in your iCloud settings.

Permissions Drift asks for

  • Music library: only if you choose to add songs from your Music library, so Drift can list and copy songs stored on your device.
  • Photos (add only): only when you save an exported video, so Drift can add it to your Photos. Drift does not read your photo library this way.

When you pick a video, cover image or file, you choose it in Apple's own picker, and Drift receives only the item you picked. When you share an audio or video file to Drift from another app, Drift receives only that file.

Purchases

Drift Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Drift never sees your payment details. It only receives confirmation from Apple that your subscription is active.

Information Apple may share

If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous, combined statistics such as crash reports and download numbers. These do not identify you.

Children

Drift does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
